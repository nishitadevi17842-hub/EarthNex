# 🌍 EarthNex – AI Landslide Risk Monitoring System

## 🚨 About the Project

EarthNex is an AI-powered landslide risk monitoring system designed to identify and monitor potential landslide-prone areas.

It combines **environmental data** such as rainfall, soil moisture, terrain, geological conditions, and historical landslide information with **camera-based visual analysis** to generate risk information and support early warning decisions.

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Landslides can cause serious damage to people, roads, infrastructure, and the environment. Traditional monitoring methods may require continuous human observation and may not provide timely information in remote areas.

EarthNex aims to provide an intelligent and accessible system for monitoring landslide risk.

## 💡 Our Solution

EarthNex uses AI to process multiple sources of information and generate a landslide risk level:

- 🟢 Low Risk
- 🟡 Medium Risk
- 🔴 High Risk

The system can also analyze camera images for visible terrain indicators and combine the results with environmental conditions.

## ⭐ Key Features

- 🤖 AI-based landslide risk prediction
- 🌧️ Rainfall and soil-moisture analysis
- ⛰️ Terrain and geological data analysis
- 📷 Camera-based terrain monitoring
- 🗺️ Dynamic risk visualization
- 🚨 Risk alerts and early warnings
- 🛣️ Safer route support
- 📡 Offline-oriented monitoring
- 💻 Designed for efficient local AI deployment
